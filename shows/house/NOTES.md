HOUSE, M.D. (Fox, 2004-2012), TVmaze show 118.

PREPARATION. Assembled by an AI assistant. The person who requested the submission asked not to review the curation beforehand, so the editorial calls below have had no second pair of eyes. Please treat the chart and tag curation as a first draft to correct rather than as checked work.

EPISODE COUNT. 176 episodes, not the 177 usually cited. TVmaze treats the season 6 premiere 'Broken' as a single 120-minute episode (6x01) where Wikipedia and most episode guides split it into 6x01-6x02. Everything here keys off TVmaze ids, so the TVmaze numbering is used throughout. This makes season 6 21 episodes rather than 22 and shifts every later season 6 episode number down by one against Wikipedia. The insignificant special 'Swan Song' (id 9207) is excluded.

SOURCE DATA. Episode ids, titles, air dates, summaries, images and ratings come from /shows/118/episodes. Guest cast comes from /seasons/:id/episodes?embed=guestcast across all eight seasons, deduplicated on (character, actor). 1,838 guest cast credits over 1,233 distinct characters; every episode has at least four. Note that TVmaze's guest cast includes generic walk-ons, and 'Nurse #2' is the single most frequent character at 127 episodes, which may be worth filtering at display time.

AXIS. Season axis, matching the other long-running shows on the site. Arcs shorter than a season are dated inside the bar label (Vogler 1x14-1x18, Tritter 3x05-3x11, the bus crash 4x15-4x16, Mayfield 5x23-6x01).

SOURCED CHART CLAIMS. The timeslot bars are derived from the air dates and airtimes in the TVmaze data itself: Tuesdays 21:00 for seasons 1-4, Mondays 20:00 for 5-7, Mondays 21:00 for most of season 8. The 'ratings peak' bar uses Wikipedia's per-season Nielsen ranks (season 2 tenth, seasons 3 and 4 seventh, against season 1 at twenty-fourth). The strike bar reflects season 4's 16-episode order during the 2007-08 WGA strike.

UNCERTAIN CHART CALLS, please check these first:
- Thirteen's bar runs seasons 4-7. Olivia Wilde was a credited regular for those seasons, but the character also appears in 8x03; the bar does not reflect that.
- Chase's second bar spans 4-8 as one span even though he is in surgery rather than diagnostics for much of seasons 4 and 5, formally rejoins the team in 6x07, and leaves in 8x20. Splitting it may be more accurate.
- Cameron's ER bar spans 4-6; her appearances in seasons 4 and 5 are intermittent rather than continuous.
- Kutner's bar ends at season 5. He dies in 5x20 and appears afterward only in House's hallucinations.
- The Dibala row (6x03-6x05) is placed under Arcs rather than under Chase; either location is defensible.

TAGS. 15 keys, 101 of 176 episodes carry at least one. Roughly 75 episodes are deliberately untagged rather than padded. 'premiere' and 'finale' are not defined here on the understanding that the site always generates them. 'Fan favorite', 'Gut-punch' and 'House in crisis' are editorial judgment and not sourced to anything; they are the likeliest thing in this submission to be wrong or to disagree with. 'Format experiment', 'Bottle', 'Two-parter' and 'Holiday' are structural and should be more reliable. Character spotlights cover only Wilson, Cuddy, Foreman, Chase, Cameron, Thirteen and Taub; Kutner, Masters, Adams and Park have no spotlight tag because their runs are short enough that a thin tag seemed worse than none.

STYLING. Accent #7b2d26 against a #2f6f8f hero gradient, Oswald for the hero font. No strong basis for these beyond the show's palette; change freely.
