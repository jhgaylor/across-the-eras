# Gilmore Girls — package notes

**Source:** TVmaze show 525 (`/shows/525/episodes`, `/episodes/<id>/guestcast`). Season axis, 7 seasons,
153 episodes, all in air order with TVmaze's own `s`/`e` numbering. TVmaze lists no specials for this show.

**Revival not included:** *Gilmore Girls: A Year in the Life* (2016) is a separate TVmaze show (17399), not part of
show 525, so it is left out.

## Confident
- Episode data, air dates, images and ratings: straight from TVmaze, unmodified apart from stripping HTML
  out of the summaries.
- Showrunners: Amy Sherman-Palladino & Daniel Palladino for seasons 1–6; they left after season 6 and
  David S. Rosenthal ran season 7. The WB for seasons 1–6, The CW for season 7.
- Almost every episode number in a chart label was checked against the TVmaze synopsis of that episode: Dean's
  first kiss (1×07) and dance-marathon breakup (3×07), his engagement (3×20) and wedding (4×04); Jess's
  arrival (2×05), getting together with Rory (3×08) and departure (3×21); meeting Logan (5×03), the Life and Death
  Brigade (5×07), 6×16, 6×20 and 6×22; Max (1×04, 1×21, 2×03); Jason (4×03–4×19); the Inn fire (3×17); Trix
  (4×16); the vow renewal (5×13); April (6×09, 6×12); Lorelai and Christopher's Paris marriage (7×08); Richard's
  heart attack (7×13); Lane's wedding (6×19).

## From memory, not the synopses
- Rory and Jess's kiss at Sookie's wedding (2×22), Lorelai proposing to Luke (5×22), Rory turning down Logan's
  proposal (7×21) and the campaign-trail job (7×22). These are well-known moments, but the TVmaze text only
  hints at them.
- "Together from 5×17" for Rory and Logan follows the 5×17 synopsis ("a new phase"); they get closer from 5×13 on.

## Tags
- Character tags (`dean`, `jess`, `logan`, `christopher`, `lane`, `paris`, `friday`) are applied wherever
  the TVmaze synopsis names that character or a Friday night dinner. One manual exception: 7×07's "Paris" is the city.
- `fanfav`, `heavy`, `milestone`, `fight`, `estranged`, `lukelorelai`, `town`, `holiday` and `birthday` are
  hand-picked. `classic` is the fallback, so every episode carries at least one tag.

## Cast
- 153 of 153 episodes have guest cast on TVmaze (1,334 credits). TVmaze lists some regulars (Dean, Miss Patty, Mrs. Kim) as guest cast in the episodes where they weren't credited as main cast; they're kept as-is.
